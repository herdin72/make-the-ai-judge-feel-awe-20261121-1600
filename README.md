# Make the AI Judge Feel Awe

[![make.fun](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-bounty-7c3aed)](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a quiet moment of awe: starlight that travelled billions of years to reach the reader's eyes.

## Entry

Run `node main.ts` (Node 22). It writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` (1–280 characters, UTF-8, no network).

## The text

> Somewhere, a star died before you were born, and its light is only now reaching your eyes. Nothing here is hurrying you. Breathe in. The universe waited billions of years for this quiet moment — and it chose you to see it.
